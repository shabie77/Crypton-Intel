📈 Multi-Timeframe RSI Engine

    BTCETHSOLXRPADA

📰 Executive Market Digest

  • Scheduling: Configurable via UTC settings (Default: 04:00 UTC / 09:30 AM IST).
  • Pinned Core Assets: BTC, ETH, and SOL stay permanently pinned as core digest coins even if tracking list is modified.
  • Market Pulse: Formula-driven 0-100 score weighing Trend, RSI, Price Change, Powell Bias, and ETF Bias.
  • Liquidation matrix: Shows Total Amount, absolute Long Value, Short Value, and Composition Ratio (%) with status labels: NORMAL / HIGH / EXTREME.
  • Macro Outlook: Includes a dedicated Upcoming Macro (24h) section showing high-impact events.
  • Curated News: Caps at Top 3 items; includes Severity Label, Source, and a "Why it matters" impact explanation.

🛠️ Full Command & Admin Suite

  • Menu Sync: Automated Telegram menu registration via setMyCommands, with admin-exclusive commands hidden from public view.
  • Operational: /digest (Immediate manual broadcast) and /macro (7-day audit checking both this week and next week sources to avoid weekend blind spots).
  • Administrative: /users, /channels, and /send (Global Broadcast).
  • Access Control: /revoke and /renew (Channel Access Management).
  • Live Config: /update_coins, /update_powell, and /update_etf (Live persistent JSON modification).

🛡️ Technical Persistence & Resilience

  • Persistence Layer: Exact file-state management for:
    config.json, rsi_state.json, news_state.json, macro_state.json, last_digest.txt.
  • Primary Storage: SQLite database tracking liquidations, alerts, users, and channels.
  • Fail-Safe Logic: Persistent retry count with linear incremental backoffs (capped at 60s).
  • Emergency Alert: Automated Admin Emergency DM sent after 3 consecutive exchange connection failures.
